Our Services

WhatsApp Chatbot

Automated, intelligent chatbots on WhatsApp Business API — handling admissions queries, fee reminders, timetable updates, and more for colleges and schools.

  • 24/7 automated responses
  • Fee & admission workflows
  • Bulk broadcast messages
  • Interactive menus & forms

RCS Communication

Rich Communication Services — next-generation SMS with images, carousels, quick-reply buttons, and verified sender branding. A superior experience without an app install.

  • Rich media messages
  • Verified business sender
  • Interactive button responses
  • Read receipts & analytics

SMS API Integration

Reliable, high-throughput SMS API for transactional and promotional messaging — water supply schedules, tax due date reminders, OTPs, and institutional alerts.

  • Transactional & promotional SMS
  • DLT-compliant templates
  • Scheduled & bulk messaging
  • Delivery reports & logs

Data Digitization

We help municipalities digitize property records, citizen databases, and land tax registers — turning paper records into structured, searchable digital systems ready for automation.

  • Property & land record digitization
  • Water supply consumer data
  • Tax register migration
  • Searchable database setup

About Us

Bluesquare Technologies

Bluesquare Technologies is a communication infrastructure company under the Bluesquare Group, registered as an MSME (UDYAM-TN-14-0073956) in Tamil Nadu.
